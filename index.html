M.S. Student · CoCEL, POSTECH · Advisor: Prof. Soohee Han

I study how robots can learn precise control from demonstrations. My recent work conditions visuomotor policies on geometric representations to reach millimeter-scale alignment in unseen environments, and I am now building teleoperation interfaces for humanoid robots.

Research Interests

  • Robot learning & imitation learning — visuomotor policies (e.g., diffusion policies) learned from real-world demonstrations
  • Precise mobile robot control — goal-conditioned, last-centimeter alignment such as autonomous docking
  • Representation learning for control — pretrained geometric and visual encoders as priors for policies
  • Humanoid teleoperation — VR and wearable-sensor interfaces for whole-body data collection
  • Interpretable robot policies — explaining what learned policies rely on when they act
